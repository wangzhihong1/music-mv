# Songs

Each song and its MV live in one stable `YYYYMMDD-slug/` folder. Do not split by status, and do not keep a separate `mvs/` tree.

Keep planning docs, `song.json`, music, character assets, analysis caches, generated shots, and finals together. Status belongs in `song.json`: `lifecycle` is `in-progress` or `completed`, and `status` is the human-readable note. Completing a song means updating those fields, not moving the folder.
